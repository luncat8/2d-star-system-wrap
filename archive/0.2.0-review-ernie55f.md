# review — ERNIE55f (e3344f6) vs 0.1.0 (c7b9289)

two unrelated histories from the same initial commit: two independent takes on the same draft.
this is the review that decided which tree 0.2.0 builds on, and what to harvest from the other.

## verdict

0.1.0 is the base. same feature set, plus the parts that are hard to retrofit: seam copies,
per-element unwrapped bearings, retarget/queue, the unrolled silhouette. it is also the only one
of the two that survives a continuity measurement. ERNIE55f contributes three small items (now in
0.2.0) and one large idea that does not exist in 0.1.0 at all — drag as a scrubbed morph — which is
a plan, not a merge.

## picked (0.2.0)

- star field: 256 static screen-space points, seeded, three alpha buckets. 0.1.0 drew a star
  system on an empty background.
- hint line under the checkbox; `touch-action: none` on the canvas.
- devicePixelRatio capped at 2.
- (findings only) drag = scrubbed morph, and which of its pitfalls survive a silhouette-collapse
  morph.

## picked later, not now

- drag-to-wrap scrub (their plan 0.3). the best idea in that branch: it answers the draft's
  deferred "pan camera drag" without a camera, by making the drag drive the same 3 s morph.
  needs the morph parameter driven from outside the timer and a clean revert to t=0.
  next plan, not a cherry-pick.
- per-class apparent radius in wrap (r = R/d·W/2π, clamped). physically motivated; 0.1.0's fixed
  type icons keep the sky legible at log scale. open question, not a defect.

## rejected

- single copy + polyline break: 0.9997 W teleports, and orbits get a visible gap at the seam.
- fixed soil rect: loses the actual circle->line morph, which is the product.
- per-frame shortest arc: 0.3853 W mid-morph jumps; the lap has to be chosen once per element.
- elliptical orbits (a, e, rot): breaks "constant distance -> horizontal line", adds tuning for
  no view-math gain.
- ground scroll on side switch: built to hide the pop of a dual from/to scene; 0.1.0's
  silhouette collapse has no such pop.
- soil colour LUT: per-frame string build replaced by one alpha crossfade.
- fly-pan camera and log1p distance: pan contradicts "projection only, 3 s morph"; log1p turns
  the line into the centre plane and loses the surface.
