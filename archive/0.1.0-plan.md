# plan 0.1 — wrap projection 2d solar system

## goal

2d view-only solar system with two projections and a seamless morph between them:

- flat: linear top-down view, camera centered on the selected body, centered on canvas.
- wrap: selected body's surface unrolled to a horizontal line at a fixed screen side —
  planet at bottom, star/moon at top. X = bearing around the selected body, scaled so the
  body perimeter exactly fits the screen width, wrapping (cylinder). Y = log altitude
  ln(d/R) above the surface, auto-scaled so every body fits on screen.

Clicking a body flies to it (3s morph) and wraps around it. A checkbox toggles
flat/wrap for the current selection. No zoom, no drag pan (deferred).

## world model (system.js)

Bodies: sun + 6 planets + 5 moons. Kinematic circles only — no physics.
Timing: Earth year = 60s, planets Kepler-scaled T ∝ a^1.5. Moon periods are real
ratios (1 day ≈ 0.164s), clamped to ≥ 2s so fast moons (Phobos, Io) don't strobe.
Sizes/distances are display-tuned, not astronomical. Table order guarantees
parent index < child index, so one forward pass updates all positions.

Per-body static reach Rmax (max distance from system origin) is precomputed:
star 0, planet a, moon a_parent + a. Used for flat-mode fit extent:
extent(c,i) = a_child for parent/child pairs, else Rmax_c + Rmax_i.
Flat scale fits extent of the selected center into min(W,H)/2 − margin — static per
center, no per-frame breathing.

## projection math (projection.js)

Canvas frame: u = wx − cx, v = −(wy − cy) (y flipped). Bearing θ = atan2(v, u).

flat(c, s):  x = W/2 + u·s,  y = H/2 + v·s. Selected body drawn as true-size disk R·s.

wrap(c):
	x = θ·W/(2π) + xoff          (perimeter 2πR maps to W for any R — equiangular)
	h = ln(d/Rc), d = |P − c|    (altitude in natural units)
	y = yLine − k·h              planet: yLine = H − 0.1H, altitude grows up
	y = yLine + k·h              star/moon: yLine = 0.1H, altitude grows down
	k = (available height − margin) / hMax, hMax = max ln(d_i/Rc) over bodies, per
	frame, clamped ≥ 1 — auto Y fit.
	soil band: 10vh thick, solid, on the fixed side of the line (brown planet,
	gray moon, dark red star).

Chirality: θ measured in canvas frame makes flat and wrap locally consistent for the
side the surface sits on (bottom for planet: near-field of flat centered low ↔ wrap
sky above the line agree). The opposite side morphs around the screen — inherent to
unrolling a circle into a line, and what the 3s morph shows.

### continuity / short-arc (the typical bug)

Raw atan2 x jumps by W at the bearing seam; per-frame per-point rounding flips
branches mid-transition and swaps screen sides. Fix:

- Every tracked element (body, orbit sample) owns a persistent unwrapped bearing θu
  around the active center, tracked per frame by shortest-angle unwrap
  d −= TAU·round(d/TAU) — a single if/else ±TAU correction fails once a tracker sits
  more than one lap from the principal value (orbiting moons do within seconds).
- At transition start θu around the new center is initialized once per element:
  n = round((x_src − x_base)/W), θu = θ + 2πn — short arc from where the element
  currently is. The ground line then rolls up toward the new body's bearing and
  unrolls from it; sides never exchange.
- xoff of the target state is frozen at start: xoff = W/2 − θ_zenith·W/(2π),
  zenith = parent body (for star: first planet), so arrival puts the parent overhead
  at screen center. Never recomputed in steady state — the sky drifts.
- Steady wrap and all transitions render every element at x−W, x, x+W (3 copies),
  offset (c − (copies−1)/2)·W so flat mode draws the central copy. Exact W jumps
  (atan2 seam, lap drift) are invisible under copies; screen-space positions are
  relifted into the central copy the same way.

### pole degeneracies

In wrap, the selected body is the pole of a log-polar map, and curves through the
pole are singular:

- The selected body's own orbit passes through the pole (d → 0, bearing flips π in
  one frame). It gets an analytic representation instead of tracking: a line buried
  one soil-depth under the surface, spanning exactly one period. Hidden in steady
  wrap; during morphs it rises out of the old ground into its real shape in the new
  state.
- Any other orbit the pole crosses over time (a selected moon crossing its parent's
  orbit circle) has samples whose bearing whips near the crossing. Samples with
  d < 1.2·R_sel are flagged singular, clamped under the soil, and skipped by the
  polyline renderer — they are behind the band anyway.
- The ground body's own icon rests on its surface line (d = 0 → y = yline), so it
  stays visible while fading during arrival.

### morph

Single blend: screen position = lerp(src(P, now), dst(P, now)), t over 3s with
smoothstep. src/dst are live projections evaluated at current body positions — pure
projection transformation, no fade, no second scene. Both x and y lerp; the wrap side
supplies x via θu trackers (up to 3 slots per element: steady src, frozen blend dst,
retarget dst).

Retargeting mid-transition: the current blend freezes as src {a, b, t} and a new
blend runs toward the new target — continuous positions, one nesting level; further
clicks queue until completion.

## rendering (render.js)

Element registry (fixed at init, preallocated Float64Array trackers, zero hot-path
allocation):

	elem 0..NB−1                bodies
	elem NB..                   orbits: 128 samples per orbiting body
	last 256                    selected-body silhouette samples

- Silhouette = closed curve of 256 samples on the body circle, drawn for every body
  that is the selected one in any live projection state (new selected emerging, old
  selected rolling up, flat disk). Positions lerp per sample, so circle ↔ line
  morphs by construction. Own silhouette x is analytic (line spanning exactly one
  period, step W/(N−1) so the ends meet mod W — no seam, no tracker).
- In a state where a body is NOT the ground, its silhouette collapses to its icon
  circle (precomputed per state per frame), so ground lines always unroll from and
  roll up to exactly the icon that represents the body otherwise — zero pop when the
  silhouette hands over to icon drawing at transition end.
- Soil band = fill between the silhouette curve and the same curve offset by
  band·(0.1H) vertically, band 0→1 as the state becomes wrap and back as it leaves.
  The band factor also cross-fades fill color (body color ↔ soil color) and icon
  alpha (1−band): flat disk is the same curve at band 0, filled body-colored.
- Orbits: polylines through the same element projection. In wrap, a circular orbit
  around the selected body becomes a horizontal line (constant h); other orbits are
  log-sine curves. Samples with d < Rc (selected body's own orbit dives under the
  surface) clamp to just below the soil band — hidden, no fade needed.
- Non-selected bodies: icon circles, radius by type (star 9px, planet 6, moon 4).
- Hit positions of all bodies are cached per frame for input hit-testing.

## input (input.js)

- Canvas click: nearest body within 14px (periodic dx under wrap) → fly to it and
  wrap (checkbox syncs to checked).
- Checkbox: toggle flat(sel) / wrap(sel) with the same 3s morph.
- Initial state: flat, sun selected, checkbox unchecked.

## validation

L0: node --check on every touched js.
L1: experiments/smoke.js — vm-loads index.html script order with canvas/DOM stubs,
drives frames through flat→wrap(Earth)→wrap(Luna)→flat, retarget, double-retarget
(queue), wrap(Saturn)→wrap(Sun)→flat, asserts: no NaN, per-frame screen travel
< 0.25 screen (catches cross-direction/long-arc bug; singular and invisible samples
excluded), wrap end-state invariants (bearing→x and log-altitude→y formulas exact,
zenith body at W/2 on departure, altitude ordering, own orbit buried off-screen,
own silhouette on the surface line, emergence from icon), flat end-state centering
and linear formula, checkbox sync. Machine-format one-line logs.
Ascii dumps of projected positions complement the smoke: they catch draw-transform
bugs (copy offsets) that pure math checks cannot see.
Manual pass list:
- click Earth from flat: soil unrolls to bottom, no side exchange, seam invisible.
- Luna line ~23% up, sun ~73%, Saturn near top; moon orbit is a straight line.
- click Luna: bottom line rolls up to icon, Luna circle unrolls to top line, Earth
  ends below it, sun repositions — all in one 3s move.
- checkbox off/on mid- and post-transition: continuous, retarget works once.
- trajectories visible in both modes, dive under soil where they cross the body.
- resize mid-transition: no crash, layout re-fits.
- 60fps with 3-copy sampling.

## decisions

- Direct wrap↔wrap morph instead of routing via flat: the draft's top/bottom scheme
  exists precisely to make it seamless; one blend, fewer phases.
- Zenith-at-center arrival: the parent body lands at screen center x when wrapping —
  predictable, and gives each transition a canonical xoff.
- Per-frame auto k (wrap) vs static extent (flat): wrap must keep the farthest body
  on screen at all times; flat fit only changes on recenter, so no wobble there.
- Own orbit in wrap is degenerate (a point physically, a curve through the pole
  mathematically): buried under the soil analytically rather than tracked — no
  whip artifacts, and it rises out of the ground naturally when morphing away.
- Moon periods clamped to ≥ 2s: real ratios would strobe (Phobos 0.05s/orbit).
