# plan 0.2.0 — view polish

## goal

Three cheap additions to the 0.1.0 wrap view, no projection math touched: a star field, a hint
line, and a device-pixel cap. The unrolled surface, the flat/wrap morph and the seam rules are
unchanged.

## star field (render.js)

- 256 points, normalized u, v in [0,1), 1-2 px, one colour, three alpha buckets 0.18 / 0.40 / 0.66.
- generated once at boot from a fixed seed; stored in Float32Arrays.
- bucket-contiguous: the draw loop therefore touches `globalAlpha` three times per frame, not 256.
- screen space: not projected, no parallax, no twinkle. resize rescales the normalized coords,
  nothing is regenerated.
- drawn right after the background fill: behind orbits, ground, bodies, labels.

## hint (index.html)

- one static line under the wrap checkbox: "click a body to wrap around it".
- plain markup, so the frame loop does no text work.
- not included: a dynamic "wrapping <name>" line. it is per-frame text for information the scene
  already shows (the selected body is the ground and the checkbox follows the target mode).

## device-pixel cap (render.js)

- backing store scale = min(devicePixelRatio, 2).
- fill cost scales with dpr^2 and the scene draws three copies. a 3x screen costs 2.25x the fill
  of a 2x screen for no visible gain at 1 px orbits and 2 px stars.

## canvas gestures (index.html)

- `touch-action: none` on the canvas: the scene owns pointer input, and a browser pan gesture must
  not steal a press. matters as soon as the press becomes a drag.

## validation

- L0: node --check on touched files.
- L1: experiments/smoke.js — star count 256, every u/v in [0,1), alpha buckets contiguous, frame
  loop still allocation-free, continuity numbers unchanged (max 0.0168 W, limit 0.25).
- manual: star density at 1280x800 and 800x600; hint legibility; no visible banding between the
  three star alphas.
