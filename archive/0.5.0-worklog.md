## fit-scale projection
changed: log-projection.js — ground state for any anchor (cameraAnchorGround), fit scale, log-space transitions.
changed: log-input.js — drag interpolates pole, bearing and zoom density to the anchor's ground state.
changed: experiments/log-smoke.js — all-bodies-fit assertions for follow, drag and body drag.
changed: index.html — hint wording.
validation: node --check x3; log_smoke result=pass fails=0; legacy smokes pass; ascii dumps of overview/follow/drag states.
decisions: scale = (G−T)·H/log(farthest extent / ref), re-fitted live every frame — a constant W/2π scale pushed far planets off-screen in follow and made empty-space drags deep-zoom into nothing; one ground state (body centre or nearest surface below) shared by click and drag.
pitfalls: a body anchor must keep the top surface point (not the grab point) 1:1 under the pointer — the grab point sits at |A−c|, which is 0 at a centre grab and breaks the log anchoring.
next: manual browser pass — drag feel from empty space, follow re-fit while flying, seam.
